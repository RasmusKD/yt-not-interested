# Privacy policy

Not Interested Button for YouTube collects no user data.

- It stores nothing and has no analytics, accounts or server of its own.
- It reads the menu data of the video cards on youtube.com in your browser, to find each card's Not interested command.
- When you click the button, that command runs in YouTube's own page code, and YouTube receives it exactly as if you had used the menu. YouTube's privacy policy covers what it does with that.
- If a card does not update after a click, the extension shows a report on the page. It stays there unless you copy it yourself, and it contains no tokens.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/RasmusKD/yt-not-interested/issues
